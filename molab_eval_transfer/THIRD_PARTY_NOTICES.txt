The AWQ Triton kernels derive from AutoAWQ/vLLM and carry the Apache 2.0 notice
in patch_awq_triton.py. Local changes preserve packed integer weights, remove
the unsafe embedding-weight output-head fallback, support Triton 3.4 syntax,
and run a nonzero numerical check before evaluation.
